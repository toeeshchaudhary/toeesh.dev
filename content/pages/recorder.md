I record myself talking. Not a podcast — no guests, no edit, no intro music. Just an hour of thinking out loud, usually while walking, usually at a time of night when nothing else is open.

The point isn't the audio. It's that saying a thing out loud forces it into a shape that thinking about it never does. The transcripts below are the useful artifact; the audio is just proof I actually said it.

Each recording has topic markers. Click one to jump to it, or open the transcript and click any line to seek there.
