I'm a 17-year-old class 12 student from Delhi NCR, and I spend most of my spare time building things.

## I'd rather show you than tell you

Instead of describing myself, I'd rather point at the work: [Navigator](/hardware), a handheld computer I'm designing; leading hardware at **ARKANOX** and R&D at **Hexoforge**; [a shelf of projects](/projects) from agri-tech IoT to terminal tools; and [toeesh.network](https://toeeshnetwork.vercel.app), my life drawn as a subway map. The common thread is making things that actually work, start to finish.

## Why engineering

Electrical and computer engineering is the one field that lets me do both halves of what I love — the physical side (circuit boards, chips, power) and the software side. My hardware work is exactly that overlap. I want to get much better at building things that can be tested, shipped, and clearly explained.

## FinStocks, and what a real product taught me

Before the current roles, I interned at **FinStocks** as an **AI developer**. It was my first time doing this kind of work inside a real company instead of on my own, and the difference was immediate: I wasn't building something to hand in, I was building features that went into the actual product and got used by real customers.

The lesson that stuck was the distance between *"it works on my screen once"* and *"it works for everyone, every day."* A real feature has to survive messy inputs it was never designed for, fail without taking down the whole app, and stay easy for the rest of the team to maintain long after you've moved on. A clever demo isn't the finish line. That standard now shows up in everything else I build, including [my hardware](/hardware).

## Why Japan

Japan is the goal because of its engineering and manufacturing culture, and its public systems — like the transit maps I keep coming back to — that shape how I think about design. It's where I'd like to take this work to the next level.

## Where I'm at

Right now I'm juggling class 12, university applications, and building real projects I can actually point to. I'm **Hardware Lead at ARKANOX**, an early-stage electrical & computer engineering startup, and **Head of R&D at Hexoforge**, an applied-research studio — both of which pull me deeper into the exact overlap of hardware and software I want to build a career in. Applications open in November, and the plan is to apply with work I can show — not just grades and good intentions. After that: study engineering somewhere serious, ideally in Japan, in 2027.
